Privacy Policy

Effective November 27, 2026 · Alkebuleum Technology LLC

Kedo is an AI network operator run by Alkebuleum Technology LLC (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information we collect through the website at kedo.im and through the Kedo admin tool, how we use it, and the choices you have.

In short: the Kedo admin tool publishes our own organization’s posts to our own social media accounts. We only use the access those platforms give us to publish and manage those posts. We don’t sell personal information or use it for advertising.

1. Who this covers

  • Website visitors to kedo.im.
  • Authorized operators who sign in to the Kedo admin tool at kedo.im/admin to manage the projects Kedo runs (such as ALKÉ Network and Alkebuleum).
  • Social media accounts we connect to the admin tool so it can publish on their behalf.

2. Information we collect

Website

The public website does not use advertising or analytics cookies and does not ask you to create an account. If you use the “Hire Kedo” form, your own email app opens with a message to us; we receive only what you choose to send. Our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) may log standard technical data such as IP address and browser type for security and operations.

Admin tool

  • Account information: the email address and password-based sign-in of authorized operators, handled by Google Firebase Authentication.
  • Content you create: post text, captions, images, videos, templates and schedules.
  • Connected platform data: when an operator connects a social media account, we receive and store the access tokens that platform issues, and basic account details needed to publish: the account or page ID, name or handle, and for YouTube the channel ID and title.
  • Activity records: a log of what was published, where, when, the links and IDs the platforms return, and any errors.

3. How we use information

  • To sign operators in and keep the admin tool secure.
  • To create, schedule and publish posts, and their first replies or comments, to the accounts an operator has connected, at the time the operator chose.
  • To refresh platform access before it expires, and to warn operators when an account needs reconnecting.
  • To keep an activity log so operators can see what was posted and fix failures.

We do not use data received from any platform for advertising, profiling, or training AI models. We do not sell it, and we do not share it except as described below.

4. Connected platforms

The admin tool uses the official APIs of Facebook, Instagram, Threads (Meta Platforms), LinkedIn, X, TikTok and YouTube (Google). It requests only the permissions it needs to publish posts and their first reply or comment, and to identify which account or page to post as. Your use of each platform is also governed by that platform’s own terms and privacy policy.

YouTube and Google

Kedo uses YouTube API Services to upload videos to a channel the operator connects. By connecting YouTube you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google’s handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke Kedo’s access at any time from your Google account security settings.

Kedo’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Where data is stored and who processes it

Admin data is stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage and Cloud Functions) in the United States. Platform access tokens and developer app keys are kept server-side and cannot be read from the browser. Service providers that process data for us:

  • Google (Firebase and Google Cloud): hosting, sign-in, database, file storage and scheduled jobs.
  • GitHub: hosting of the kedo.im website.
  • The social platforms listed above, which receive the posts we publish to them.

We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users or others.

6. Retention

  • Access tokens are kept while an account is connected and deleted when it is disconnected.
  • Posts, media and logs are kept until an operator deletes them, or for as long as the project needs them. Deleting a post from the admin tool does not remove it from the platform it was published to.
  • Operator accounts are deleted when that person no longer needs access.

7. Data deletion

To remove Kedo’s access to a social account and delete the data we hold for it:

  1. In the admin tool, open the project → Connected accounts → Disconnect. This deletes the stored access tokens immediately.
  2. You can also revoke access on the platform itself (for example Facebook Settings → Business integrations, Google account permissions, or the apps settings on LinkedIn, X or TikTok).
  3. To ask us to delete any other information about you, email hello@kedo.im. We will confirm and complete the deletion within 30 days.

8. Security

Admin access is limited to authorized operators. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest by our providers, and security rules prevent browsers from reading tokens and keys. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect the information we hold.

9. Children

Kedo’s website and admin tool are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal information, or to object to how we use it. To exercise these rights, email hello@kedo.im.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows the latest version, and significant changes will be noted on this page.
